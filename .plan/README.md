## Direction (options for the maintainer, not ranked against the bugs)

1. **Tell the owner when a run fails, outside the app, and let a PARTIAL run resume.** Evidence: `MEMORY.md` records "a PARTIAL run cannot be resumed; no email or webhook alert and no error workflow" (`.plan/workflow-engine-n8n-alignment.md`); on 2026-11-16 the staging run `01a1e267` failed at 09:00 local time and the only signal was the in-app header warning; the brain has no mail or webhook sender (a search for mail and webhook libraries in `apps/brain/src` finds none). Trade-off: needs a provider choice, per-owner preferences and a rate limit so a bad night does not send hundreds of mails; a first version could notify once per workflow per day.
2. **Give the read worker a per-user turn.** Evidence: `MEMORY.md` "Read worker fairness (accepted 2026-11-01)": `DataReadService.due()` takes 20 reads per tick across all users, oldest first, so one 1,000-company import delays everyone else's drawer reads by minutes; the import cap is 1,000 (`IMPORT_CAP` in `linkedInRecord.ts:213`). Trade-off: it was accepted until it hurts; with more than a handful of owners it will, and the change is small.

## Findings considered and rejected

- People import issues one write per profile (`people.service.ts:895-927`): bounded by `IMPORT_CAP = 1,000`, one-off, so not worth batching.
- "CI does not run integration tests" (reported by one audit pass): wrong. `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs the brain and spider integration suites; what is stale is the documentation (plan 005).
- "Add a browser end-to-end suite to CI": decided. The body has no DOM test setup by design and e2e tests are run by hand (`CONTRIBUTING.md:76`).
- Positive-reply checks are selected without a claim, so two job runs could call the AI for the same check (`positiveReply.service.ts:249-368`): the budget ledger entry is keyed by the check id (a repeat never pays twice), and the worker is one replica, so the cost is at most a duplicate cheap call.
- Split `packages/contracts/src/workflow.ts` (8,279 lines) and other large modules: real, but a large refactor with no bug behind it; revisit when a change there is blocked by its size.
- Body pins `typescript ~6.0.2` while the rest uses 7 (`apps/body/package.json:58`): no documented reason and no evidence of harm; investigate only when the body type check is slow or TypeScript 7 is needed there.
- Nothing consumes the `scraping.results` queue (declared in `apps/spider/src/messaging/topology.ts`): known, capped at 10,000 messages and expires after a day; not worth a change.
- `/cdn` SSRF guard: reviewed, it refuses private addresses at connect time and looks sound.
- Per-user cap on paid data reads and the cross-tenant "already used" answer when adding a LinkedIn account: recorded decisions in `MEMORY.md`.

## Direction (options for the maintainer, not ranked against the bugs)

1. **A second source of leads: search people by who they are.** The chat already asks for job titles, places and sizes, but the only finder in the campaign is "people who commented on or reacted to a post about your problem", which needs the owner to guess phrases and whose daily supply swung from 38 to 0. The catalog has `collect.searchPeople` (about $0.004 per person plus $0.10 per 25, i.e. about $0.008 a person; `workflow.ts:3483`). The posts finder measured about $0.026 per invite-eligible lead (quoted: $3.22 for 124 qualified or maybe over 56 posts). The fit rate of a title search is unknown. Trade-off: steady supply and no phrases question versus leads who showed no interest signal. Start as a spike: run one title search on a staging campaign and compare the qualified share.
2. **Tell the owner when finding or connecting stalls.** The invite slowdown on staging was found by hand (`MEMORY.md`, 2026-11-15). A "no new leads in N days" or "accounts have room but sent nothing today" line in the dashboard's Needs attention tab would catch it. It needs the same read as plan 006's backlog count (`insights.capacity.waiting`).
3. **Add the follow-up steps to a connect-only campaign later** (an "Add follow-up steps" action) once plan 008 lands; see its maintenance notes.

## Findings considered and rejected (this audit)

- "Replace the six workflows with one": the engine runs one workflow at a time per workflow and chains by design; a single graph would make pause, run history and limits coarser. Two workflows (find, connect) is the right split.
- "Drop Discover's profile read": it is what makes the Score meaningful (`.plan/campaign-finds-nobody.md`, 173 in and 2 out before it); keep.
- "Start Discover and Invite on one clock only": rejected for the chain's benefit on a good day; plan 006 keeps the chain and adds the clock.
