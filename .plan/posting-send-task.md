# Posting task: publish a post with its files and give back the post link

- Status: done, except step 7 (the first live post)
- Created: 2026-10-25
- Locked by user: yes, 2026-10-25 (all six answers as proposed)

## Goal

Make "Post now" and "Schedule" on the Write a post page real. A new spider task, `linkedin-post`, types the owner's post into the account's own LinkedIn page, attaches its photos, video or document (from the bucket keys the draft already holds), presses Post, and answers with the link of the new post, which the page then shows so people can open and watch it. Brain queues it like a reply, never twice, and keeps the result (state and link) with the post.

## Requirements

Confirmed (from the request):

- A task that posts, supports the files, sends, and as a result gives the post link.
- The link is something people can open and watch (a public post).

Proposed answers (the open questions below ask the owner to confirm each):

- The task acts on the signed-in owner's own account, one post at a time per account, at a person's pace, within limits stated below, never in bulk. A post is typed once: after Post is pressed a repeat of the command never types again (it answers unsure).
- The link comes from LinkedIn's own success state ("Post successful, View post") and, when that is missing, from the newest post on the account's own activity page whose first words match. It is a link of the form `https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:<id>/`, checked by a strict pattern in the spider and again in brain. Never invented.
- The post is public (visibility "Anyone"), because the point is that people can watch it. Other audiences are a later choice.
- Text and files come from a frozen copy: pressing Post now moves the draft into a publication that keeps the text and the file keys, so a later edit or delete of a draft cannot change or remove what is being posted.
- Schedule becomes real: a scheduled publication waits for its time, and a brain worker job dispatches it when due, under the same limits, office hours and browsing budget as a post sent now.

Locked answers (owner, 2026-10-25):

1. Scope: sending now and scheduling are built together.
2. Limits: at most 3 posts in 24 hours per account and at least 1 hour between two.
3. Files: deleted from the bucket once the post is up; those of a failed or unsure post stay for a retry.
4. Recording first: yes. A capture run that posts nothing, then one real test post made by the owner by hand (text, one photo, one PDF) and deleted afterwards. Build starts only when both recordings are shared.
5. Link not found: the publication ends as "Posted" with a note and a pointer to the account's activity page.
6. Duplicates: the same words from one account within 7 days are refused with a sentence.

## Scope and non-goals

- In scope:
  - Contracts: the task's input and report shape, `POST_LIMITS`, the pattern of a post link, a `PostPublication` state set.
  - Spider: task `linkedin-post` (capture mode, then the real run), `context.postFiles.fetch` built on the generalised `fetchUploads` (path `posts/<user>/<file>/<name>` from the command's user and the file ids, sizes and types checked again by kind), reader and selectors for the composer, success state and the link, tests with synthetic markup and a real Camoufox run against a stand-in page.
  - Brain: table `PostPublication` (additive migration), `PostPublicationService` (send now, schedule, cancel a scheduled one, list, back to draft, retry), hook `linkedInPost.hooks.ts` (`onRequest` account `ACTIVE`; `onResult` state, link, failure sentence), worker jobs `post-send` (dispatch due and queued ones, one per account at a time) and `post-sweep` (ends one stuck too long as unsure, deletes the files of posted ones), router `post` (`publish`, `schedule`, `cancel`, `get`, `list`, `backToDraft`), tests (unit and integration).
  - Body: Post now and Schedule enabled behind a confirmation that says it posts to LinkedIn for real, with the account, the words and the attachment; a status area for the publication (queued, posting with a step line, posted with the link and a copy button, failed with the sentence and Back to draft or Try again, unsure with what to check); the drafts list shows scheduled and posted items apart from drafts; links are only shown as links when they pass the strict pattern.
  - Docs: `MEMORY.md`, `deploy/gcp-setup.md` (the spider worker's role must read `posts/`: it has `objectAdmin` on the bucket already, so the check is only written down).
- Out of scope: polls, mentions that resolve to people, tagging, a first comment, editing or deleting a post on LinkedIn, reading reactions and comments back, posting as a company page, an audience other than public, reposting.

## Edge cases and risks

- Never twice: `markAttempt` right before Post; a delivered-again command, a timeout after Post, a lost proxy or a crash all end as unsure and never retype. Brain never retries a publication by itself; "Try again" is the owner's choice and says the post may already be up.
- Unsure is common: LinkedIn may show nothing we can read. The page says "check your LinkedIn activity", gives the link of the account's own activity page, and a later account read does not settle it (posts are not read back), so this state needs a manual "It is up" or "It is not up" choice from the owner (small and explicit, no guessing).
- Not a real post: composer buttons disabled, a character limit hit, a file LinkedIn refuses (its own scan, type or size, unverified), a document that needs a title, a video still processing: the task ends `FAILED` with a fixed sentence and clears the composer (best effort) so the next run starts clean; the files and the publication stay so the owner can fix and retry.
- Long uploads: a document of 100 MB or a video of 512 MB through a proxy may not finish in the limit; the run ends before Post (safe, nothing posted) with a sentence. Not measured.
- Link read: the toast can be missed (it fades); the fallback reads the account's own activity and matches by the first words and the time, and refuses to guess when two posts match. A wrong link would send people to someone else's post, so no match means no link.
- Public means public: the confirmation says the post is visible to anyone; the text, files and link are never logged, and the run recording (video, kept 7 days) shows the post being written, like the reply's.
- Frozen copy and files: the publication owns the file keys until it ends; draft saves cannot delete them (they act on drafts only); on POSTED the files are deleted (Q3), on FAILED or UNKNOWN they stay; removing a LinkedIn account removes its publications' files with its drafts' files, failing the removal when a file cannot be deleted, as for drafts.
- Scheduled time: stored as an instant with its zone; a time in the past when the job runs is sent at once only if it is under 15 minutes late, else marked "missed" and left for the owner; the account's office hours, daily browsing cap and gap apply, and a refused dispatch waits for the next tick rather than failing.
- Limits and compliance: per-account post limit (Q2), one post at a time per account, the browsing budget of `.plan/account-browsing-budget.md`, human pacing in the composer, no bulk and no automatic posting without the owner's confirmation each time (a schedule is the owner's own decision, shown in the list and cancellable until it starts).
- Concurrency: two clicks on Post now, two tabs, a schedule firing while the owner posts now: one publication per draft (unique), one run per account at a time (the reply's lease), a second request answers with the sentence of what is already going.
- Security: every call owner-scoped; the task input names file ids, never keys; the spider builds `posts/<user>/<id>/<safe name>` from the command's user; names must already be safe; the link is accepted only if it matches the pattern; the report item holds only the link.
- Existing data: additive migration; drafts without keys (older ones) cannot be posted (Post now explains: attach the files again); the planned time stays on drafts.
- Deploy: new task needs the worker image to announce it (a worker started before the task existed never announces it; brain then refuses with "unavailable"); the brain migration runs in the init container; the new jobs are announced to the cron monitor (`title` and `purpose`).
- Rollback: Post now and Schedule are disabled again by one line; the table and the task can stay unused.
