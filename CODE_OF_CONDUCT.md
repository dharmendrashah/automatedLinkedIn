# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want automatedLinkedIn to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, background, disability, gender identity, experience level, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and kind. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers; no question is too basic.
- Credit others for their contributions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, or using the project to promote abuse of LinkedIn or its users.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsible Use

This project automates professional outreach. Contributors and users are expected to:

- Respect LinkedIn's Terms of Service and applicable laws (including privacy and anti-spam regulations).
- Never use the project for scraping personal data at scale, deception, impersonation, or unsolicited bulk messaging.
- Be transparent and honest in any outreach sent with this tool.

Contributions designed to evade platform protections or enable abuse will not be accepted.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public space where someone represents the project).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

To report a violation, email **dharmendrashaheagle@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.
